How to use this map

📍 Situation Near Me uses your current location to summarize flood susceptibility and nearby verified information.

🌧 In Flood Risk mode, click a location inside Western Province.

The popup can show:
• Flood susceptibility
• District, DSD and GND
• Nearest historical hotspot
• Verified reports within 5 km
• Nearest health facility

📍 Verified Reports displays approved community observations.
